# ECGrav 1.19.0 — Effective sample sizes for the complex MCMC, and faster exact samplers

`RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC` spaces its samples one correlation time apart, and that does not
make them independent. This release has the driver report how many independent samples a run is
worth. It also removes a trap that could stall the driver's equilibration at large M, documents all
four of its call forms, and fixes three smaller bugs in it. Separately, the facet-labeled and
unlabeled exact samplers set up and draw much faster, with unchanged distributions.

---

## ⚠️ Read before upgrading

**Nothing breaks.** No argument and no returned key is removed. `RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC`
returns two new keys, and a call that matches none of its four forms now returns `$Failed` with
`::argerr` instead of staying unevaluated. Requires Wolfram Language 15.0+, unchanged.

**Seeded MCMC runs change.** The equilibration's high-vertex replica starts from a different
complex (see below), so a seeded `RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC` run need not reproduce 1.18.0's
samples. The exact samplers' distributions are unchanged, and seeded unlabeled draws are unchanged.

## Effective sample sizes: `RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC`

```wolfram
seed = {{1, 2, 3}, {1, 4, 5}, {2, 4, 6}, {3, 5, 7}};      (* p = 3, M = 4 *)
edges = Length[Union @@ (Subsets[#, {2}] & /@ #)] &;
{meas, info} = RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC[seed, {edges}, 2, 200];
info["corrT"]                  (* sweeps between samples *)
info["effectiveSampleSize"]    (* use this, not 200, for error bars *)

RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC[info, {edges}, 2, 200];        (* continue the run *)
RandomPureSimplicialComplexMCMC[seed, True, {edges}, 2, 200];  (* fixed vertex set *)
```

- **Samples are not independent.** They are corrT sweeps apart, and corrT is about one decay time
  of the slowest observable. Consecutive samples stay correlated, at 0.2–0.3 in the energy and the
  vertex count at p = 3, M = 8 and 12, which about doubles the variance of a sample mean. The
  documentation used to call them independent.
- **`info["effectiveSampleSize"]`** is the smallest over the recorded observables, and
  **`info["effectiveSampleSizeValues"]`** gives one per observable, in the order of
  `info["corrTValues"]`. Each is n/(2τ), with τ the integrated autocorrelation time of the returned
  samples over Sokal's self-consistent window.
  - A constant observable gives `Missing["Constant"]`.
  - Fewer than 20 samples give `Missing["TooFewSamples"]`.
- **All four call forms are documented**, in the usage message and on the reference page. The
  vertex count is free, or fixed with `True` after the seed. Each form starts either from a seed
  complex or from the association a previous run returned, which skips equilibration and the
  correlation-time estimate.
  - Each measurement row is {k, energy, vertexCount, op1, …, opm, complex}.
  - On the fixed-vertex chain the third entry is the number of edges instead.

## Equilibration no longer starts in a trap

The replica that probes the high end of the vertex count started from M disjoint facets. Under the
unlabeled weighting (labelingChoice 2) that complex's automorphism group, (p!)^M M!, is so large
that moves out of it are almost never accepted.

- **The exit time outgrew the budget.** The mean first exit is 339 sweeps at p = 3, M = 12, 1,629
  at M = 20 and 26,250 at M = 50, beyond the whole 25,000-sweep equilibration budget.
  Equilibration cannot converge until all four replicas agree. A run at M = 50 would have taken
  about a day and failed roughly 40% of the time.
- **The replica now starts from a linear chain** of facets, consecutive facets sharing one vertex
  (v = (p−1)M + 1), whose automorphism group is small. At p = 3, M = 20 it reaches typical vertex
  counts within about 25 sweeps, where the disjoint start was still trapped after 600.

## Faster exact samplers

- **`RandomUniformFacetLabeledPureSimplicialComplex[{p, M}, k]` sets up 25× faster** with the vertex
  count free: 6.2 s instead of 155 s at p = 4, M = 30. It now takes the whole row of vertex-count
  weights from `NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes[p, M, nlist]` in one pass and memoises it per (p, M).
  `NumPCClearCache` clears it.
- **`RandomUniformUnlabeledPureSimplicialComplex` draws 4–230× faster per sample and sets up 3–11×
  faster.** At (p, M, n) = (3, 30, 27) a sample takes 4.3 ms instead of 0.56 s, and the step-1
  weights take 7.2 s instead of 81 s.
  - **Grouping.** Candidate orbits are grouped by the lengths of the uncovered cycles they meet,
    which is all the completion count reads. Each orbit's key is a packed integer updated in place.
  - **Memoisation.** |Fix(ρ)| is memoised: at n = 27 the step-1 weights requested it 240,840 times
    for 14,742 distinct values.
  - **Orbit lists.** Each cycle type's orbit list is built in linear time instead of quadratic:
    0.35 s instead of 2.9 s for the identity type at p = 4, n = 34.

## Fixed

- **The equilibration trap** described above.
- **Fewer than five samples issued `Mod::indet` at every sample.** The progress interval
  `Floor[numSamples/5]` was 0. It is now at least 1.
- **A `Sow` inside an operator added rows to the samples.** The driver gathered its samples with an
  untagged `Reap`, so a `Sow` from an operator, or from a `Print` hook, landed in the measurements.
  All four forms now use a private tag.
- **A call matching none of the forms came back unevaluated and silent.** It looked like a missing
  overload. Two common slips were `True` after the operator list instead of before it, and
  `{Length}` instead of `{Length[#] &}`. Such calls now return `$Failed` with `::argerr`, which
  lists the accepted forms.

## Known limitations

- **The unlabeled sampler with a free vertex count is impractical at large (p, M).** Its setup is
  estimated at about 7,400 h at (4, 30), and it is memory-bound. See `UnlabeledSampler.md`.
- **1.18.0's Wang–Landau limitations still apply.** The first 1/t phase can stall on rare levels at
  n ≳ 21, and single-edge moves change topology slowly.

## Verified

- **The effective sample size** against an AR(1) series with a known autocorrelation time. New
  tests also cover the new keys and the chain start.
- **The chain start.** The exit times above are exact, computed from the weights of every proposal,
  and measured exits at M = 12 confirm them. Three equilibrations at p = 3, M = 20 converged at
  1,201–1,601 sweeps.
- **The faster samplers.**
  - The facet-labeled row matches the per-n counts, including the zeros below the smallest
    feasible n.
  - The unlabeled sampler's packed keys track the uncovered cycles under any covering order.
  - Its completion counts satisfy the one-step identity the draw relies on.
  - Its orbits partition the p-subsets.
- **`NumUnlabeledPureComplexes`** has a third independent oracle, built on `CanonicalGraph`.
- **The driver fixes** have six new tests: one each for the `Mod::indet` and `Sow` fixes, and four
  call-form slips for the new catch-all.
- **The usage example above** was run against the release source.

Suite **325 → 339 tests**, all green.
