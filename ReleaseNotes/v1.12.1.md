# ECGrav 1.12.1 — the free-energy solve says when it did not converge

`ComputeMinusBetaTimesFreeEnergy` returned silently on hitting its 30 000-iteration cap. A caller
then held free energies worse than the tolerance implied, with nothing to say so — and everything
reweighted from them inherited that quietly.

One added message. No signature changed, no return shape changed, nothing removed, and the free
energies themselves are unchanged. Requires Wolfram Language 15.0+, unchanged.

---

## ⚠️ Read before upgrading

**Nothing about your numbers changes.** This release adds a warning and nothing else. If your
solves were converging — and at the 1.12.0 default of `1e-8` on a well-overlapped run they converge
in a couple of hundred iterations — you will never see it.

**If you do start seeing it**, the free energies you were already getting were the ones being
described. The message is new; the condition is not.

---

## What it looks like

```
ComputeMinusBetaTimesFreeEnergy::noconv: The MBAR free-energy self-consistency
solve did not converge within 30000 iterations. The estimated error remaining in
the returned free energies is 0.0280940, against a tolerance of 1.*^-8. They are
returned anyway and are the best available, but they are a lower-quality estimate
than the tolerance implies, and everything reweighted from them inherits that.
Poor overlap between neighbouring states is the usual cause -- the iteration
converges slowly when few samples carry weight at more than one state -- so check
the effective sample sizes before raising the iteration cap.
```

**The free energies are still returned.** This is a warning about their quality, not a failure.

**Raising the cap is usually the wrong response.** The iteration converges slowly when few samples
carry weight at more than one state, which is an overlap problem in the run, not a budget problem
in the solver. `MBAREffectiveSampleSize` is the thing to look at. 1.12.0 made the convergence
estimate trustworthy — it is a geometric extrapolation of the error remaining, not the raw step —
so the number in the message is a real estimate of how wrong the free energies are.

## Why it matters more than it looks

`ComputeMinusBetaTimesFreeEnergy` produces the `minusBetaF` that every other MBAR routine consumes.
An under-converged solve propagates silently into extrapolated expectation values, effective sample
sizes, and the thermodynamic metric a `GraphCTLSchedule` is built from. 1.12.0 made the error
estimate available; this makes something look at it.

---

## How this was verified

- The warning fires on a deliberately starved iteration budget.
- **The negative control is the leg that matters**: the same data with a real budget must be
  silent, so the test fails if the condition is ever inverted or the threshold made vacuous.
- The free energies are still returned alongside the warning, not swallowed.
- Removing the warning fails the suite.

The suite is at **196 tests**, all passing.

## Install

```wl
PacletInstall["/path/to/ECGrav-1.12.1.paclet"]
```

Requires Wolfram Language 15.0+, unchanged from 1.7.0.

Upgrading in a running session needs a `Quit[]` first — `Needs` is a no-op while `` ECGrav` `` is
already in `$Packages`, so the kernel keeps the old definitions.
