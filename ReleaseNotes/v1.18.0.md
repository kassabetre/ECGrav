# ECGrav 1.18.0 — Wang–Landau sampling of the density of states

Whether a transition is first or second order is a question about the density of states g(E).
Canonical sampling answers it badly: the states between two peaks of P(E) are exactly the ones a
Metropolis chain at the transition temperature avoids.

This release adds Wang–Landau flat-histogram sampling for the package's graph Hamiltonians, together
with the thermodynamics and the transition-order diagnostics that read g(E). It also gives
`NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes` whole rows over the vertex count and a faster total, and it fixes
how the specifications' maths renders on GitHub.

---

## ⚠️ Read before upgrading

**Nothing breaks.** There are three new public symbols and one new overload. No existing symbol
changes behaviour, and no number moves: `NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes[p, M]` returns the same values
as before, faster. Requires Wolfram Language 15.0+, unchanged.

**One correction to the 1.17.0 notes:** `HHomogeneousSource` cannot be passed to a driver directly,
as those notes said. See *Fixed* below.

## Wang–Landau: `GraphWangLandau`

```wolfram
h[am_List, p___]  := HHomogeneousSource[am, p];      (* inert heads in your own context *)
dh[am_List, p___] := delHHomogeneousSource[am, p];

res = GraphWangLandau[seed, h[3., -0.5, 0.5, 1., 0.7], dh[3., -0.5, 0.5, 1., 0.7],
        {0, 50}, 8*10^6];                             (* seed: any graph whose energy is in the window *)
dos = res[[2]];                                       (* "logg", "levels", "normalisation", ... *)

ThermodynamicsFromDOS[dos, Range[0.3, 3., 0.1]]       (* U, Cv, -beta F, Binder cumulant per beta *)
TransitionOrderDiagnostics[dos, Range[0.3, 3., 0.01]] (* every local maximum of P(E), per beta *)
```

The ensemble is labeled, so g is a plain density of states, and no automorphism order is ever
computed. Three design decisions were forced by measurement rather than chosen.

- **Levels are keyed by energy, not indexed on a lattice.** The spectrum is lattice-valued but
  sparse: at n = 5 the 27 realised levels occupy 2.92% of a 925-point lattice. A dense array would
  hold slots the walk can never visit, and the flatness test could then never pass.
- **The energy is accumulated incrementally, not recomputed.** A full H costs 6–20× the incremental
  delta at n = 21. Float drift would otherwise register as spurious new levels, so a proposal
  landing on an unseen key is re-anchored from the Hamiltonian. `"levelMerges"` reports when that
  happens, and a periodic assertion against a fresh evaluation catches a wrong `delH`.
- **The normalisation is the total state count**, Σg = 2^C(n,2), not g(Emin) = 1. The labeled ground
  level is degenerate: g(Emin) = 60 at n = 5.
  - **Windowed runs.** A run that rejected proposals at the window edge reports
    `"normalisation" -> "WindowRelative"`. Canonical averages are unaffected, but −βF then carries an
    unknown constant.

**Two schedules.** The default, `"OneOverT"`, follows Belardinelli and Pereyra: its first phase
halves ln f whenever every level has been visited since the last reset, and it then switches to
ln f = nLevels/step. `"Classic"` (f → √f) is the textbook baseline. At n = 5 Classic drove ln f to
1.9·10⁻⁹ and still had a 10.3% maximum error, so a small ln f under the naive schedule is not
accuracy. Budget a `"OneOverT"` run with `maxSteps`, since its `"FinalLogF"` is arithmetically a step
budget.

**The callbacks need inert heads in your own context.** A package function such as
`HHomogeneousSource[e0, …]` without the matrix is not inert: its catch-all turns it into `$Failed`
before the driver sees it. The two wrappers above stay unevaluated until the driver supplies the
adjacency matrix.

## Reading the density of states

- **`ThermodynamicsFromDOS[dos, betas]`** gives U, Cv, −βF and the Binder energy cumulant
  1 − ⟨E⁴⟩/(3⟨E²⟩²) at each β. Cv is the total, not per site.
- **`TransitionOrderDiagnostics[dos, betas]`** reports every local maximum of P(E) = g(E)e^(−βE), not
  an argmax. Where P is bimodal it also gives the peak separation and the log depth of the minimum
  between the peaks.
  - **Prominence filtering.** Candidates are filtered by topographic prominence, and the threshold
    is an option. Attainable energies are irregularly spaced, so P(E) is jagged: a bare
    local-maximum test found 31 "maxima" in the *exact* n = 6 density and called every β bimodal.
  - **Scan the threshold.** A genuine double peak holds at two peaks across thresholds; lattice
    granularity does not.
  - **Only across sizes.** Both the separation and the depth growing with system size is the
    first-order signature. Neither means anything from a single size.

## Facet-labeled counts: whole rows over n

- **`NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes[p, M, nlist]`** (new) returns the count for every vertex count in
  `nlist`. It is computed from one pass over the short-cycle multiplicities by (8.1) of
  `FacetLabeledCount.md`, rather than one cycle-type table per n. It is elementwise identical to
  mapping the scalar form.
- **`NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes[p, M]`**, the total over n, is now built from a single table, since
  the padding identity telescopes the sum. It is 7.0× faster at (5, 6) and 14.5× at (2, 20), and its
  results are unchanged.

## Fixed

- **`HHomogeneousSource`'s usage message** said it was "directly usable by the single-parameter
  drivers". It is not, for the reason given above, and the message now says so. The same sentence
  in the 1.17.0 notes is left as published.
- **The specifications' maths renders on GitHub.** GitHub's Markdown escaping inside `$..$`
  consumed backslashes before punctuation, so `\{` and `\}` vanished. Every set literal rendered as
  a bare comma list, with no error to warn anyone. Repo-wide it ate 866 braces, 348 `\;`, 264 `\,`
  and 21 `\#`.
  - **The fix.** 522 maths spans across 13 specifications now use the ` ```math ` and `` $`..`$ ``
    forms, and `md2pdf.py` normalises both. `\operatorname`, which GitHub's KaTeX refuses, became
    `\mathrm`.
  - **The PDFs were never affected.**

## Known limitations

- **The first 1/t phase can stall on rare levels at n ≳ 21.** It halves ln f only when every
  registered level has been revisited. On rugged spectra some levels are found once and almost
  never again, and ln f then stays at 1 (seen for 10⁷ steps).
  - **Workaround:** narrow the window to exclude such levels, or split the range into overlapping
    windows.
  - **Planned:** a first-phase test over active levels only.
- **Single-edge moves change topology slowly.** Where each closed-surface topology is its own energy
  level, a window spanning several of them may not converge at n ≳ 21. Use narrower windows.

## Verified

- **`GraphWangLandau` against exact enumeration.** At n ≤ 5 it recovers every realised level, the
  exact normalisation and the exact ground-state degeneracy of 60, with zero drift and zero spurious
  levels.
- **`ThermodynamicsFromDOS` against direct summation over every state.** At n = 5 and 6 they agree
  to 10⁻¹³ on U, 6·10⁻¹¹ on Cv, 10⁻¹⁴ on the Binder cumulant and 3·10⁻¹⁴ on the free energy.
- **`TransitionOrderDiagnostics` against a synthetic density built so the answer is known.** It
  recovers the peak positions, separation and depth to float precision, and reports no bimodal β
  on a single Gaussian.
- **Against independent canonical chains at n = 10.** U agrees at all ten β.
- **Two defects found before release.** The n ≤ 5 validation could not see them; both were found at
  n = 10 and fixed:
  - out-of-window levels were registered and never visited;
  - the 1/t schedule's first phase never ran.

  Each has its own regression test.
- **`NumFacetLabeledPureComplexes`.**
  - The fast total matches the previous definition on all 77 (p, M) pairs checked.
  - The row form matches the scalar map elementwise.
  - A new test pins the padded Stirling identity three independent ways.

Suite **293 → 325 tests**, all green.
